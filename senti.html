Senti AI

Finance intelligence that reads markets, flags risk before it compounds, and explains its reasoning in plain language.

In development

Most financial models tell you what changed. Senti tells you why it matters and what tends to happen next, and shows its working.

An analyst should be able to trust a result or challenge it. Senti is built so that every flag arrives with the reasoning behind it, in language a person can act on.

Senti is a Kenya-first financial platform. The language model does the explaining; the figures come from a deterministic computation layer, so a number is never improvised by the model.

What it is built to do

  • Signals and exposure

    Real-time monitoring of market signals and of an organisation's exposure to them.

  • Explanations with every flag

    Each flag comes with its reasoning in plain language, so an analyst can accept it or push back.

  • Figures you can repeat

    Calculations run in a deterministic layer, so the same inputs give the same numbers every time.

  • Fits the desk

    Designed to sit inside the systems a finance desk already uses, not to replace them.

Where it stands

  • In development, ahead of its first public demonstration.
  • Built in Nairobi by Ouroboros Tech.
