Privacy

Privacy notice.

Plainly: we collect what you send us so we can reply, and nothing else. No tracking, no advertising, no selling data.

What we collect

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive the name, email address, and message you choose to send. That's it.

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. We don't run third-party trackers or build profiles of visitors.

What we use it for

Only to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to continue a conversation you started. We don't add you to a marketing list without you asking.

Who we share it with

Nobody, other than the infrastructure providers needed to deliver the site and our email. We don't sell or rent personal data.

This site is served over GitHub Pages, and fonts are served by Google Fonts, both of which necessarily receive your IP address in order to deliver the page.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as they're commercially useful and then deleted. You can ask us to delete yours sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@ouroboros.tech and we'll action it.

We process personal data in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Changes

If this notice changes materially we'll update this page. Continued use of the site after a change means the updated notice applies.
