Research

Research that has to ship.

We do applied research at the edges of blockchain, AI, and data, then hold it to a harder test than a benchmark: it has to survive contact with a real institution. The lab and the product are the same building.

A result that can't be deployed, explained, and trusted isn't finished research to us. It's a promising start.

Plenty of good ideas never leave the paper. Our advantage is the opposite problem: we get to test hypotheses against production systems, real data, and users who will tell us plainly when it doesn't work. That feedback loop is the research method.

What we're working on

Open problems we're chasing

These are directions, not press releases. We share specifics when there's something real to show and the people involved are happy for us to.

01

Explainable financial intelligence

Models that don't just flag risk but reconstruct the reasoning behind the flag, so a human can audit the machine instead of trusting it. The core of Senti.

02

Hybrid consensus & settlement

Protocols that hold public verifiability and private, regulated performance in the same ledger, with guarantees you can actually prove.

03

AI for real-world, low-resource data

Methods that work on the messy, sparse, code-switched data African institutions actually have, not the clean corpora benchmarks assume.

04

Privacy-preserving computation

Getting value from sensitive financial and public-sector data without pooling it into a single point of failure or trust.

05

Reliability at scale

What it takes for a system to keep its promises under real load, partial failure, and the long tail of production reality.

06

Human-in-the-loop systems

Interfaces where an expert and a model each do what they're best at, and the handoff between them is legible.

Why it works here

The lab feeds the product. The product feeds the lab.

Research proposes; production disposes. A hypothesis that survives a live deployment earns its place in the next product; a product that strains against its limits sets the next research question.

It's the same closed loop the company is named for, applied to how we learn. Nothing we discover sits on a shelf, and nothing we ship stops being studied.
