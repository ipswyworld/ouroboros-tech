Safiri

A travel marketplace for Kenya that puts checked local guides and drivers in front of travellers, with the price shown before they decide.

In development, not yet open to the public

Land in Kenya and be looked after by someone who has been checked.

Safiri is built by Ouroboros Tech. It is two apps and a back office: travellers use Safiri to find, compare and book local people and to keep a trip organised; providers use Safiri Partner to build credibility, manage availability, answer bookings and get paid.

Guides, hosts, translators, drivers, cultural companions and experience hosts each keep their own category, so a traveller always knows who they are dealing with.

What it is built to do

  • Checked providers

    Licences are checked, and the proof is shown to travellers at the point where they decide.

  • Costs in view

    Fees are shown separately and each service says what it includes. Cancellation and refund terms appear before anyone commits.

  • A trip in one place

    Calendar, map, payment status, flight details and currency conversion sit beside the bookings.

  • Location only with consent

    Sharing a location is opt-in and limited to active bookings.

  • Made for the phone in your pocket

    Mobile first, designed for low to mid-range Android devices, with large touch targets and reduced-motion support.

Where it stands

  • In development. The traveller app, Safiri Partner and the admin tools are being built and tested.
  • Android first, iPhone to follow.
  • Not yet open to travellers or providers.

What we will publish

Guides signed up, bookings made and payouts delivered are the numbers that matter for a marketplace. We will publish them here when they are real.
