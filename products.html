Flagship · Artificial intelligence

Senti AI

Finance intelligence that reads markets, flags risk before it compounds, and explains its reasoning in plain language.

Most financial models tell you what changed. Senti tells you why it matters and what tends to happen next, and shows its working, so an analyst can trust it or challenge it rather than take it on faith.

  • Real-time signal and exposure monitoring
  • Plain-language explanations behind every flag
  • Built to sit inside the systems a desk already uses

Chain

Ouroboros Hybrid Blockchain

Our own settlement layer, the openness of a public chain with the speed and privacy a regulated institution actually needs.

Public where trust must be earned in the open; private where performance and confidentiality are non-negotiable. One ledger, two modes, engineered so institutions don't have to choose.

  • Faster clearing and settlement for regulated flows
  • Selective privacy without leaving a shared source of truth
  • Interoperable with the rails partners already run
